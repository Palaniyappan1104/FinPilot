# FinPilot

**A Multi-Agent AI Platform for Financial Research and Investment Decision Support**

FinPilot is a final-year engineering project designed to provide AI-assisted financial research and investment decision support.

---

> ### ⚠️ Disclaimer
> **FinPilot is NOT a trading bot and does not execute automated trades.** It does NOT guarantee investment returns or provide certified financial advice. All analysis, reports, and insights generated by the platform are strictly for research, informational, and educational decision-support purposes.

---

## Overview

FinPilot orchestrates multiple specialized AI agents to analyze company fundamentals, technical indicators, market sentiment, valuation models, and risk factors, delivering cohesive investment research dossiers.
